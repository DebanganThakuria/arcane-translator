# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. This is a small project about reading novels; there is nothing here
worth being unpleasant over.

## What is expected

- Assume good faith. Most disagreements are misunderstandings.
- Critique code, not people.
- Accept that maintainer time is limited and unpaid. A pull request may sit for
  a while, or be declined, without it being personal.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, or deliberately derailing
discussions. Publishing someone's private information without permission.

## Reporting

Report problems to the maintainer through
[private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/DebanganThakuria/arcane-translator/security/advisories/new)
or by email. Reports are handled privately.

Maintainers may edit, hide or delete contributions that break these rules, and
may block accounts that repeat them.

## Attribution

Adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
shortened to fit a project of this size.
